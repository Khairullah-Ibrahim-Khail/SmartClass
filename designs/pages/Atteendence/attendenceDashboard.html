Welcome to the Attendance Dashboard

Here you can manage and monitor student attendance efficiently. Please follow the guidelines below to ensure accurate and up-to-date records:

Attendance Advisory:
  • Update attendance for every class daily to maintain accurate records.
  • Check for absent or late students and follow up promptly.
  • Use the Edit section to mark or correct attendance.
  • Review attendance analytics in the Reports section for trends or irregularities.
  • Students should ensure they are marked present in the system and report discrepancies immediately.

Tip: Consistent attendance tracking helps improve class performance and ensures all students receive proper guidance and support.
