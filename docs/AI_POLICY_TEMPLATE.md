# AI use at [School name] — policy template

*A starting text. Read it, change what does not fit your school, then give it to teachers, students and parents. It is not legal advice; if your country or ministry has rules on children's data, check them first.*

## 1. In one paragraph
[School name] uses AI inside SmartClass to help teachers write lectures and quizzes and to give students a study helper. AI helps people; it does not replace the teacher, and it never decides marks, attendance or discipline.

## 2. Who may use it
- **Teachers**: may use AI to draft lectures, quizzes and explanations. The teacher reads and corrects every text before students see it. The teacher is responsible for what is published.
- **Students**: may use the AI Tutor to understand lessons and practise. It is a helper for learning, not a way to hand in work that is not their own — the rules on cheating in exams and assignments stay the same.
- **Students under [age, e.g. 13 / 16]**: [choose one: may not use the AI Tutor / may use it only with parent consent / may use it with a teacher present]. The administrator can switch AI off for the whole school (Settings › AI providers); the school must enforce the age rule above itself, because the system has no age check.
- **Administrators** decide which AI provider is used and can switch AI off completely.

## 3. What is sent to the AI provider
Only what is needed for the answer: the text of the lecture, the class name and the question that was typed. **Not sent:** the person's name, e-mail, marks, attendance or photo.
The provider is [name of provider, e.g. a commercial service / a server of the school]. A commercial provider receives the typed text on its own servers, in another country. Because of that:
- Do not type private information (home address, phone numbers, health problems, family problems) into the AI.
- Teachers should not paste student names or marks into prompts.

## 4. Who can read what students ask the AI Tutor
Only the student. Teachers and administrators cannot read a student's tutor conversations. One safety exception: if a message suggests a student may be in danger (for example talk of self-harm), the first part of that message is shown to the teachers of the class so they can help. Tell students this when you introduce the Tutor. (The safety check can be switched off by the administrator in Settings › Privacy & data.)
Tutor conversations are deleted automatically after [12] months (the school can change this in Settings › Privacy & data).

## 5. Quality and honesty
- AI can be wrong, even when it sounds sure. Check important facts, numbers, names, dates and code before you rely on them. Answers in Pashto and Dari may need more checking than English.
- Do not copy AI text as your own work when the task asks for your own work. If AI helped, say so.
- Do not use AI to make fake messages, pictures or claims about other people.

## 6. Parents
Parents may ask the school: which AI is used, what is sent, how long conversations are kept, and to have a child's AI conversations removed (the school administrator can do this from Settings › Privacy & data). Parents are told about this policy at [enrolment / start of term].

## 7. Problems
If the AI gives an answer that is wrong, rude or unsafe, tell your teacher. The teacher or administrator can report it to [contact] and, if needed, switch AI off while it is looked at.

## 8. Review
This policy is read again every [year] and whenever the AI provider changes.
